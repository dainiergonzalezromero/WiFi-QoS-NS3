AI-based WiFi QoS Optimization in NS-3

CA-SDWN-WiFi-QoS is an open-source research framework for optimizing Quality of Service in IEEE 802.11 wireless networks using Software-Defined Wireless Networking, Knowledge-Defined Networking, Random Forest prediction models, and Bayesian Optimization.

Research-grade simulation framework for intelligent wireless QoS.

Designed for technical and academic experimentation with AI-assisted QoS management in Software-Defined Wireless Networks.

📡

IEEE 802.11e EDCA QoS

Models differentiated WiFi traffic classes including voice, video, best effort, and background traffic to evaluate QoS-aware network behavior.

🧠

AI-driven optimization

Uses Random Forest prediction models and Bayesian Optimization with Optuna to tune EDCA parameters for improved network performance.

🕸️

SDWN/KDWN architecture

Integrates Software-Defined Wireless Networking concepts with knowledge-driven control for adaptive QoS management.

Automated experiments
